Meet Nifemi

The unusual food scientist.

I’m a food scientist and storyteller making conversations about food safety, nutrition, and public health easier to understand.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Food Science and Technology from the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta. Through relatable stories and practical education, I help people connect what they eat with how they live.
